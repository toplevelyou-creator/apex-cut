Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who we are

Apex Cut is a desktop video-editing app run by the creator behind toplevelyou (contact: toplevelyou@gmail.com). It is used only to publish videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data Apex Cut accesses

When you connect an account, Apex Cut uses the official sign-in of each platform (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram) and receives only what the requested permissions allow:

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and set their details (title, description, privacy, schedule) on your channel, and your channel's basic info.
  • TikTok: your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload or post videos to your account.
  • Instagram: your basic account info and permission to publish content to your professional account.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and app credentials are stored only on your own computer, inside Apex Cut. They are used only to publish the videos you choose to publish. Your videos and project files stay on your computer except when you publish them to the platform you select.

Apex Cut does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not send it to any server operated by Apex Cut.

Google API Services

Apex Cut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Apex Cut uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect an account in Apex Cut at any time, which deletes its stored token from your computer. You can also revoke access from each platform: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions, and Instagram under Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites.
