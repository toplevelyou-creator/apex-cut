Apex Cut

Apex Cut is a desktop video editor for the creator brand toplevelyou. It cuts silences and bad takes, adds captions and motion graphics, and publishes finished videos to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Apex Cut is used only by its owner to publish to his own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
